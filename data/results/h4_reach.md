## The reach and the criterion

- One hour of wall time is reached at **d\* = 123.3 digits** (interpolated between RSA-120 and RSA-130).
- **H4 rejected**: d\* does not lie in [127, 137], 5 digits around the 132 that H1 carried down by L_N[1/3, 1.923] gives.
- The effective constant that takes one hour at d\* to H1's 4,300 to 4,700 hours at 200 digits: c_eff = 1.666 to 1.683, against 1.923.
- From d\* to 200 digits is 77 digits; at 200 digits the laptop needs 4,300 to 4,700 times its hour.
- Across the runs, ln T rises 1.406 per unit of the L_N[1/3] exponent (the local constant), against 1.923.
- RSA-100 to RSA-110: × 2.80 in wall time, doubling every 6.7 digits (L-scaled: × 5.03).
- RSA-110 to RSA-120: × 3.25 in wall time, doubling every 5.9 digits (L-scaled: × 4.62).
- RSA-120 to RSA-130: × 3.13 in wall time, doubling every 6.1 digits (L-scaled: × 4.29).
