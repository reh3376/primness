## Questions this run answers

- Does the harness reproduce a Dickman-scale smoothness count on a real NFS polynomial? Yes: predicted densities are of the right order at all four bounds, and the counts are far above Poisson noise.
- Is the raw algebraic excess over Dickman statistically real on this region? Yes, by 100 to 250 standard errors.
- Is that excess evidence for D1? No. Applying the Murphy α computed here removes it on the algebraic side; the residual is at the per-mille level and changes sign across bounds. D1 would need an excess that survives α, and on this instance there is none.
- Is the rational side understood? Not fully. After α it is still below ρ(u + α/ln B) and above the local-density form; the Dickman approximation's own accuracy at u near 2 is the limiting factor, not the measurement.
- Do the algebraic and rational sides behave independently? See the both-sides observation above.
