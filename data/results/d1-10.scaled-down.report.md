The implied shift from the data (-5.25 mean) and the computed α (-4.71) agree.
- On the rational side the computed α is +0.5695 and the α-adjusted gap is still -6, -12, -19, -29 standard errors: the linear form's values are less often smooth than ρ(u + α/ln B) predicts by about 14% of the density. The local-density form ρ(u) + ρ'(u)/ln B overshoots the other way (+5, +9, +15, +18 standard errors; algebraic side +3, +6, +10, +16). The count sits between the two textbook approximations, which bounds how precisely Dickman itself can be trusted at u near 2 with B = 2^20: the model uncertainty here is of the order of the residual, not the Poisson noise.
- The rational side is less smooth than Dickman at every bound; its implied α is +1.13 to +1.18. A linear form over coprime pairs is not a random integer of its size, and the correction has the opposite sign.
- Within B = 2^20, binned by u in steps of 0.1 (bins with at least 1,000 values): the excess ratio is 2.35 at u ≈ 3.4 and 3.05 at u ≈ 5.5. The excess is not a constant factor; it rises with u, as a shift in ln|F| predicts, because ρ falls faster at larger u.

- Algebraic density per b row at B = 2^20: mean over the first quarter of b (1..375) is 0.00208, over the last quarter (1126..1500) is 0.00026. Larger b means larger values, so density falls across the region; the comparison with Dickman already accounts for size value by value.

## Questions this run answers

- Does the harness reproduce a Dickman-scale smoothness count on a real NFS polynomial? Yes: predicted densities are of the right order at all four bounds, and the counts are far above Poisson noise.
- Is the raw algebraic excess over Dickman statistically real on this region? Yes, by 100 to 250 standard errors.
- Is that excess evidence for D1? No. Applying the Murphy α computed here removes it on the algebraic side; the residual is at the per-mille level and changes sign across bounds. D1 would need an excess that survives α, and on this instance there is none.
- Is the rational side understood? Not fully. After α it is still below ρ(u + α/ln B) and above the local-density form; the Dickman approximation's own accuracy at u near 2 is the limiting factor, not the measurement.
- Do the algebraic and rational sides behave independently? See the both-sides observation above.
