## The statistic and the criterion

- The sieving phase is 76.7% of RSA-130's wall time in H4 (5,902 of 7,695 s).
- s = 0.849 × 0.767 = **0.651**: the sieve proper's share of the complete factorization.
- A sieve that cost nothing and found the same relations: **2.87 times** faster at most.
- **H5 holds**: s < 2/3 (a ceiling of 3 times).
- A relation collection that cost nothing at all (sieve and cofactorization): 4.29 times at most.

## At 200 digits, from H1

- The sieve proper is 54 to 69% of `las`'s counted time over H1's six ranges.
- With relation collection at 83 to 91% of the total (the records' share), a free sieve makes the factorization 1.8 to 2.7 times faster at most, and a free relation collection 5.9 to 11.1 times.
