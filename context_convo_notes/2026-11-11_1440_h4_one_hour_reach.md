## Why

H1 measured the cost at the goal's size: 3,896 laptop-hours to collect RSA-200's relations. The roadmap carried every figure at another size from that point by L_N[1/3, 1.923], including the size at which the laptop's cost falls to one hour. H4 measures that size directly: the largest hard number this laptop factors completely, end to end, in one hour.

## What was built

- `docs/h4_falsifiable_prediction.md`, committed alone in 4660e12 before any measured run. H4 holds if the one-hour reach lies within 5 digits of 132, the reach H1 implies under L_N[1/3, 1.923].
- `scripts/h4_reach.sh` runs `cado-nfs.py N server.address=localhost -t 18` on RSA-100, RSA-110, ... in empty work directories under `hw-sample`, ascending until a run takes more than one hour. Each run uses CADO-NFS's default parameter file, with nothing overridden. The `server.address` setting keeps CADO-NFS's work server on the loopback interface.
- `scripts/h4_summary.py` provides N from the published factors and checks the printed factors against them. It filters the logs, taking out paths, network addresses and certificate hashes. From the timestamps it computes the phases, then the reach and the criterion, plus per-phase hardware statistics.

## What was measured

| instance | wall time | measured / L-scaled |
|---|---:|---:|
| RSA-100 | 4:31 | 10.0 |
| RSA-110 | 12:38 | 5.5 |
| RSA-120 | 41:00 | 3.9 |
| RSA-130 | 2:08:15 | 2.8 |

- The reach is 123.3 digits, interpolated between RSA-120 and RSA-130, so H4 is rejected.
- The time doubles every 5.9 to 6.7 digits. L_N[1/3, 1.923] says every 4.3 to 4.8.
- The effective constant from the reach to H1's 200-digit cost is 1.67 to 1.68. Fitted across the four runs alone, it is 1.41.
- Sieving takes 60 to 77 percent of the time, and the linear algebra 13 to 17 percent. At RSA-130 the matrix has 1.18 million rows.

## Questions answered

- How large a number does the laptop factor in one hour today? 123 digits with CADO-NFS as shipped. That is 77 digits short of the goal, or 4,300 to 4,700 times its hour.
- Does L_N[1/3, 1.923] carry H1's cost down correctly? No. Below 200 digits it makes the cost fall too fast, and the 240-to-260-digit figures are restated with the effective constant beside it.

## Questions raised

- How much slower is a sustained 200-digit run than H1's 20-second samples? By the clocks, about 18 percent.
- How far do tuned settings move the reach: the linear algebra's threads, the relation target, polynomial selection effort and the power mode?
- The linear algebra at 200 digits, which H3's rates also need.

## Hardware

AC power, automatic power mode. During sieving the CPU was 96 to 99 percent active at RSA-110 to RSA-130, at 37 to 44 W of CPU power. macmon's 6-core tier held 2.88 GHz and its 12-core tier 3.9 to 4.1 GHz. The CPU peaked at 86.7 °C, during RSA-130.
